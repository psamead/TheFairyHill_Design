Evidence: current `TechnicalArt/LESSONS_CHARACTER_ANIMATION.md` and resume; the earlier whole-character candidates failed in hands and identity details. 2. **A chosen preview is not a finished gameplay feature.** Keep static art, preview timing, animation, effects, file transfer, integration, and Play Mode acceptance separate. Evidence: the 2026-11-18 resume and cross-action rules. This avoids turning a visual selection into an unrequested combat-system expansion.
